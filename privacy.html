Comprehensive Privacy Policy for FitFlow

Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Application Name: FitFlow
Developer / Organization: M. K. T. Rumeshika (FitFlow Redesign Project)

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to FitFlow. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how FitFlow collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application and related services (including AI-powered workout plans, camera-based nutrition tracking, and real-time social challenge rooms). This policy complies with international data protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) alignment principles regarding user wellness records.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized and frictionless fitness experience, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Account & Profile Data: Information provided during registration via Firebase Authentication, such as your email address and authentication tokens.
  • Biometric & Fitness Data: Workout completion logs, schedule availability, active streaks, and milestone progression data.
  • Camera & Media Data: Images captured via your device camera strictly for real-time barcode scanning and AI meal-photo macro estimations. These images are processed securely and are not permanently stored on external servers beyond the immediate estimation window.
  • Social Activity Data: Challenge room participation, shared milestone summaries, and synchronized leaderboard rankings.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for core app functionality and service enhancement:

  • Generating adaptive, AI-driven workout recommendations that match your daily schedule and available equipment.
  • Processing food images and commercial barcodes to instantly estimate nutritional value and macro-nutrient distributions.
  • Managing real-time peer challenge rooms and maintaining synchronized live leaderboards via secure web sockets.
  • Improving app performance, diagnosing crashes via monitoring tools, and reducing onboarding drop-off friction through progressive profile creation.

4. Data Security & Storage Compliance

  • Encryption: All user profiles, biometric history, and nutrition logs are protected using industry-standard AES-256 encryption protocols both in transit and at rest.
  • Infrastructure: Core structured data is stored in secure relational databases (PostgreSQL), while real-time social states are managed through encrypted cloud channels (Firebase/Firestore).
  • Access Control: Strict role-based access control (RBAC) ensures that third parties or unauthorized users cannot access personal fitness information. Professional coaching overrides are strictly regulated through secure administrative gateways.

5. Your User Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Under data protection laws like the GDPR, you retain full rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a full export of your stored fitness and account data at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update or correct your profile metrics and customized goals through the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request complete deletion of your account and raw data records from our secure storage servers.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke permissions (such as camera or social sharing access) through your device settings or app preferences.
